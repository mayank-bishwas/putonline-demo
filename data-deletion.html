User Data Deletion Instructions

For iSellOnline DMbot · Last updated: 25 October 2026

iSellOnline operates DMbot, a tool that automates responses to comments on Instagram.

How to request deletion

If you want to request deletion of information processed by DMbot, email hello@isellonline.in with the subject line DMbot Data Deletion Request.

Include your Instagram username and enough information to help identify the relevant interaction. Do not send your password or Instagram access token.

What happens next

We will review the request, verify any information reasonably necessary to process it, and delete eligible data associated with the request within a reasonable period, subject to applicable legal requirements.

Some information may need to be retained temporarily where required for security, fraud prevention, or legal compliance.

Contact

For questions about this process, contact hello@isellonline.in.
