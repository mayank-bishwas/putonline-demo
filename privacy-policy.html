Privacy Policy

For iSellOnline DMbot · Last updated: 25 October 2026

1. About this service

iSellOnline operates DMbot, an Instagram automation tool designed to respond to comments containing configured keywords by sending a relevant private message and, where enabled, a public comment reply.

2. Information processed

When the service operates, it may process Instagram comment text, comment and media identifiers, timestamps, and information needed to respond to a comment. It may also process technical information needed to validate webhook requests, prevent duplicate responses, and record whether a reply was successful.

3. How information is used

Information is used to identify comments containing configured keywords, associate comments with the relevant post, send configured replies, prevent duplicate responses, troubleshoot errors, and maintain basic service records.

4. Instagram and third-party services

The service uses Meta's Instagram APIs and webhook infrastructure. Information handled through those services may also be subject to Meta's applicable terms and privacy policies.

5. Data retention and security

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to operate, secure, and troubleshoot the service. Access tokens and application secrets should be stored securely and are not intended to be included in public logs or webpages.

6. Sharing

Personal information is not sold. Information may be processed by Meta and other service providers where necessary to operate the service or comply with applicable law.

7. Your choices and deletion requests

You may ask questions about information associated with your interaction with this service or request deletion by contacting us using the details below, subject to applicable legal obligations.

8. Contact

For privacy questions or deletion requests, contact hello@isellonline.in.

9. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as the service changes. The latest version will be published at this URL.
